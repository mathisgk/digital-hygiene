Briefing 04 | Threat class: account recovery abuse

Email, account recovery, and breach monitoring

Mission objective. Protect the one account that resets all the others.

Field report

  • Email is the master key. Every password-reset link lands there. Lock it down first: unique password, key or passkey, and review the recovery phone and recovery email.
  • Use aliases. Apple Hide My Email (iCloud+), SimpleLogin, and Firefox Relay give each site its own address. A breached alias is burned, your real address is not.
  • After any scare, look for attacker persistence: forwarding rules, filters, app passwords, connected third-party apps, and signed-in devices.
  • Turn on breach notifications at haveibeenpwned.com for every address you own. When one fires: change that password, confirm it is unique, and confirm MFA is on.
  • Keep school or work email out of personal accounts, and the reverse.

Myth versus reality

Myth“A breach alert means I was hacked.”
RealityIt means a company was. Your exposure depends on whether that password was unique.
