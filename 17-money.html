Briefing 17 | Threat class: identity theft and fraud

Money and identity protection

Mission objective. Put a lock on the thing thieves actually want.

Field report

  • Freeze your credit at Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. It is free, it blocks new accounts opened in your name, and you can lift it when you need credit.
  • In the US, get an IRS Identity Protection PIN to block fraudulent tax filings.
  • Turn on bank and card alerts for every transaction. Use virtual or single-use card numbers for sketchy checkouts.
  • Payments that cannot be reversed (gift cards, crypto, wire transfers, “send it to my agent”) are what scammers ask for. No legitimate agency or employer does.
  • Peer-to-peer apps: make the feed private, and only pay people you know.
  • If your identity is stolen, report at IdentityTheft.gov and file with your bank.

Myth versus reality

Myth“Credit monitoring protects me.”
RealityMonitoring tells you after. A freeze stops it before.
