Briefing 01 | Threat class: everything

Mitigation, threat modeling, and defense in depth

Mission objective. Swap the question “am I safe?” for “what am I protecting, from whom, and what will I do about it?”

Field report

  • Perfect security is impossible. The job is to stop the avoidable hacks (phishing, password reuse) and limit the damage from the unavoidable ones (a company you use gets breached).
  • Five threat-model questions: What do I want to protect? Who do I want to protect it from? How likely is it that I will need to? How bad is it if I fail? How much trouble am I willing to go through to prevent it?
  • Baseline (most people): password manager, MFA, updates, phishing sense, backups. Elevated (money, a business, a public profile): add security keys, a credit freeze, a locked-down phone number, separate browser profiles. High risk (journalists, activists, anyone targeted by a person who knows them): add strict Signal habits, Lockdown Mode, separate accounts for separate roles, and get specialist help.
  • Defense in depth: prevent, detect, recover. No single tool does all three.
  • Least privilege: do daily work from a standard account, not an admin account. Give every app and extension only what it needs.
  • Herd immunity: your habits protect the people who text, email, and share files with you.

Myth versus reality

Myth“I'm not important enough to hack.”
RealityMost attacks are automated. You get hit because your password was in a leaked list, not because of who you are.
