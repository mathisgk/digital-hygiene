Briefing 19 | Threat class: active compromise

Incident response: I think I got hacked

Mission objective. Know the first hour.

Field report

  • 1. Contain. Disconnect the device from Wi-Fi. Do not pay anyone, do not reply to anyone.
  • 2. Switch devices. Use a clean device for everything that follows.
  • 3. Email first. Change the password, sign out all sessions, check forwarding rules, recovery info, and connected apps.
  • 4. Then the rest: password manager, bank, Apple or Google account, social. Confirm MFA on each.
  • 5. Money. Call the bank, freeze credit, watch for new accounts.
  • 6. Warn contacts if someone could be impersonating you.
  • 7. Clean or rebuild. If malware ran (a ClickFix paste, a cracked installer), assume the device is compromised. Back up documents only, wipe the OS, and reinstall.
  • 8. Report. FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov, FBI at ic3.gov for serious cases, your school or employer IT.
  • 9. Learn. What was the entry point? Fix that, not just the symptom.

Myth versus reality

Myth“Antivirus said it is clean, so it is fine.”
RealityCredential theft leaves nothing for antivirus to find.
