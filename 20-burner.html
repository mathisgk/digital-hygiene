Briefing 20 | Threat class: unknown files

The burner OS and what comes next

Mission objective. See how to open risky things without risking your real machine.

Field report

  • A virtual machine is a disposable computer inside your computer. Use one for suspicious files, unknown software, and risky browsing. Take a snapshot, do the risky thing, revert.
  • Setup: download VirtualBox from virtualbox.org. The installer may warn about missing Python dependencies (the Python bindings need extra packages). You can skip that unless you plan to script the VM.
  • How to escape a full-screen VM: the Host key is Right Ctrl by default. Host+F toggles full-screen, Host+Home opens the menu.
  • A VM is not a force field. VM-escape bugs exist, so keep the host updated and do not log in to real accounts inside it.
  • Future talks: whether to turn telemetry off at all and where it is worth it, alternate operating systems (Kali for the security labs), and virtual-machine networking.

Myth versus reality

Myth“A VM makes anything safe to open.”
RealityIt lowers the risk. It does not remove it.
