Briefing 09 | Threat class: unpatched software

Updates, patching, and end-of-life

Mission objective. Treat “update available” as a task, not a notification.

Field report

  • Patch everything: OS, browser, apps, router firmware, connected gadgets. Unpatched software is how most automated attacks get in.
  • Automate updates, and also check by hand. Do not wait for devices to get around to it.
  • End-of-life is a deadline. Windows 10 support ended October 14, 2025. Microsoft extended consumer Extended Security Updates through October 12, 2027, and those cover security fixes only. Treat it as a bridge to Windows 11, or to Linux if the hardware cannot run it.
  • Phones age out too. Know how many more years of updates your model gets. iPhones and Pixels lead.
  • Smart devices that never get updates: do not buy them, or isolate them on the guest network.
  • Delete apps and extensions you do not use. Fewer pieces, less to patch.
  • Cracked software and pirated games are a leading route for credential-stealing malware.

Myth versus reality

Myth“Updates just change the layout.”
RealityMost are security fixes, and attackers read the patch notes too.
